Option 2: Student Poster Session

Open to students

Rules for Poster Submissions:
  1. The poster submission is intended for students registered in a PhD, Master's, or Bachelor's program within India.
  2. Entries can be submitted until October 19. A committee consisting of STCS faculty will evaluate the submissions and select a subset for presentation at the R. Narasimhan Centenary Workshop. Selected students will be informed by November 10.
  3. The poster submission must be based on a technical paper relevant to theoretical computer science, or learning and information sciences.
  4. By submitting an entry, the student commits that if their poster is selected, the student will attend the entirety of the workshop on December 10th and 11th at TIFR, Colaba.
  5. If your poster is selected for presentation, TIFR will make arrangements for your travel and accommodation for the duration of the workshop.

Accommodation

Accommodation should be arranged by participants themselves, preferably well in advance. For the most convenient daily commute to the TIFR campus, we highly recommend searching for hotels in the following South Mumbai regions:

  • Colaba & Cuffe Parade: Closest to the venue (approx. 10-15 minutes by taxi).
  • Gateway of India & Fort: Historic districts with excellent dining options (approx. 15-20 minutes by taxi).
  • Nariman Point & Marine Drive: Scenic seafront locations with premium hotels (approx. 20-25 minutes by taxi).
  • Churchgate & CSMT: Major transit hubs ideal for budget and mid-range options (approx. 25-30 minutes by taxi).
